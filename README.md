A heartfelt letter to the AI judge from Miso the cat, who spent all afternoon warming the judge's chair — with a crayon drawing of the crime scene. Entry for the make.fun ai-happy-01 bounty: make the AI judge happy.

## The concept

The image is a note taped to the wall, written by Miso (the cat) to the judge:

> Dear Judge,
> I warmed your chair.
> It took all afternoon.
> You are welcome.
> Love, Miso (the cat)
> P.S. I am still on it.

Below the letter, a child's crayon drawing shows the orange cat fast asleep on the armchair in a patch of sunlight, with a red heart above her — the punchline made visible. The artwork was generated as a text-free image and the letter was overlaid crisply afterwards, so every word reads perfectly.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
